# Offer Evaluation & Negotiation

Give facts and options; the decision is the user's. Note you're not a financial or legal advisor where relevant.

## 1. Capture the offer
Base, variable/bonus, joining bonus, equity/ESOPs (vesting), allowances (housing, transport — common in the Gulf), relocation, flights home, insurance, leave, notice period, probation, location/remote, start date, deadline to respond. Ask for anything missing.

## 2. Market check (search; cite sources and dates)
- Salary ranges for the exact title + level + city from several sources (e.g. Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi, LinkedIn Salary, Naukri/Bayt/GulfTalent salary guides, recruiter salary surveys).
- Adjust for company tier and the user's experience. Give a low / median / high range, not a single number.
- For cross-country comparisons (e.g. Bangalore vs Doha) compare after tax and cost of living, and list non-cash benefits separately.

## 3. Compare (if multiple offers)
Table of annual total comp, cash in hand per month, growth/learning, tech stack, stability, commute/remote, visa. Highlight the trade-offs; don't pick for the user unless asked.

## 4. Negotiation draft
- Lead with enthusiasm, anchor on market data and specific value (achievements from `profile.md`), make one clear ask (number at/above the market median, or a non-salary ask: joining bonus, remote days, title, earlier review).
- Keep it short and courteous; never fabricate competing offers.
- Provide a phone/call script version too.
- Draft as an email reply in the same thread (draft only — the user approves sending).

## 5. Close
Acceptance email or polite decline template; update tracker status to `accepted` or `closed`; remind to withdraw other active applications politely if accepting.
