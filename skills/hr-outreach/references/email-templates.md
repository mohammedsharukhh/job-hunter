# Email templates (personalise every bracket; keep ≤150 words)

## 1. Application by email
Subject: Application: [Exact Job Title] — [Full Name]

Dear [Name / Hiring Team at Company],

I'm applying for the [Job Title] role ([Job ID if any]) posted on [source]. I'm a [headline] with [X years] building [relevant thing].

Two things that match what you're looking for:
- [Achievement 1 mapped to a must-have, with a number]
- [Achievement 2 mapped to a must-have, with a number]

[One sentence on a genuine company detail — product, recent launch, tech stack — and why it interests you.]

My tailored resume is attached. I'm available [notice period] and happy to talk at your convenience.

Best regards,
[Full Name] · [Phone] · [LinkedIn]

## 2. Recruiter / hiring manager intro (LinkedIn or email)
Hi [Name], I saw you're hiring for [Job Title] at [Company]. I've [one-line strongest match, with a number]. I've applied via [portal] — would you be open to a quick look at my profile? Thanks, [Name]

## 3. Follow-up (reply in same thread, after ~7 days)
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name], following up on my application for [Job Title]. I'm still very interested and can share anything else that helps — portfolio, references, or availability for a call. Thank you for your time.
[Name]

## 4. Interview confirmation
Hi [Name], thank you — I'm glad to confirm the interview on [day, date, time, timezone] via [mode]. Please let me know if there's anything I should prepare. Best, [Name]

## 5. Thank-you after interview (same day)
Hi [Name], thank you for speaking with me today about [Job Title]. I enjoyed discussing [specific topic] and I'm excited about [specific aspect]. Looking forward to the next steps. Best, [Name]
