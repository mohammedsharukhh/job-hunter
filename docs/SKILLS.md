# All 18 skills, in plain language

You never need to remember skill names — just talk to Claude normally. This page explains what each one does.

**Legend:** 🔁 runs automatically · 💬 when you ask · 📧 needs email connected · 🌐 uses your browser

---

## 🚀 Getting started

### quick-tailor 💬
**Tailor one resume to one job — no setup.**
Say: *"tailor my resume to this job: …"* (attach your resume)
You get: a tailored resume to download, keyword match score (before → after), honest list of missing skills, optional cover letter.

### job-hunt-setup 💬
**One-time setup.** Reads your resume, asks a few questions, creates your private workspace and turns on the automatic schedules.
Say: *"set up job hunter"*

---

## 🔁 The daily loop

### job-hunt 🔁
**The whole day in one go.** Checks replies → finds jobs → tailors resumes → prepares applications → shows you one approval table → sends approved ones one by one → updates your dashboard.
Runs automatically each day. You can also say *"run my job hunt"*.

### job-watchlist 🔁
**Be among the first applicants.** Every hour, checks the job boards of companies you chose (those using Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable or SmartRecruiters) and alerts you to new matching jobs within hours of posting.
Say: *"watch Razorpay and Careem for iOS jobs"*

### find-jobs 🔁 🌐
**Searches everywhere else** — job-board connectors, LinkedIn, Naukri, Bayt, GulfTalent and company career pages. Skips duplicates, old "ghost" listings and scams. Gives every job a fit score (and learns from jobs you reject).

### tailor-resume 🔁
**A new resume for every job.** Rewrites your resume around the job description's keywords using only true facts from your profile, in one of 3 clean layouts. Then two automatic checks:
- **ATS self-test** — reads the PDF back like hiring software; must score ≥ 85
- **Fact check** — blocks any number, skill, employer or certificate you don't actually have

### apply-to-job 🔁 🌐
**Fills in the application form** on the company's portal with your tailored resume. Asks you about anything it doesn't know (salary, visa…). Submits only after your approval.

### hr-outreach 🔁 📧
**Emails the hiring team.** Uses only official contacts published by the company. Short, personal email with the tailored resume attached. Sent one by one after you approve the day's list, with a daily limit and at most one polite follow-up.

### check-updates 🔁 📧
**Reads your inbox for you.** Sorts replies into confirmations, tests, interviews, rejections and offers, updates your tracker and drafts responses.
Say: *"check my job emails"*

## 🎯 Getting the job

### interview-prep 💬
Company research, likely questions (HR, technical, DSA, your own resume), answers built from your real experience, and a **mock interview with scores**.
Say: *"prep me for my Acme interview"*

### referral-finder 💬 🌐
Finds your LinkedIn connections and alumni at a company and drafts a short referral request.
Say: *"find me a referral at Zoho"*

### offer-negotiation 💬
Checks an offer against market salaries for that role and city, compares offers, and drafts a polite negotiation email and call script.
Say: *"I got an offer from X — is it fair?"*

### skill-gap-coach 💬
Finds the skills you keep missing across the jobs you want and gives you a short learning plan with small projects to prove them.
Say: *"what skills am I missing?"*

### linkedin-profile 💬
Writes a recruiter-friendly LinkedIn headline, About section and skills list.
Say: *"improve my LinkedIn"*

---

## 📊 Tracking & learning

### job-dashboard 💬
A visual page: applied, response rate, interviews, offers, what needs action, follow-ups due.
Say: *"show my dashboard"*

### job-insights 🔁 (weekly)
Learns which job sites, resume layouts and email styles get you replies, and suggests changes.
Say: *"what's working?"*

### daily-brief 🔁
A short morning summary on your phone.
Say: *"send me a job brief every weekday at 8:30"*

### share-stats 💬
Optional: share your anonymous results (counts and rates only) to help publish real success rates for everyone.
Say: *"share my job-hunter stats"*
